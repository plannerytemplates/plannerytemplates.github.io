Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Plannery Templates handles on this website and in the Plannery Pin Scheduler, a small tool we use to post Pins to our own Pinterest account. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Plannery Templates is a small shop selling printable planners on Etsy, run from Poland, in the European Union. Contact: plannerytemplates@gmail.com.

This website

This site has no sign-up forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Fonts and images are served from this site itself, not from third parties.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Plannery Pin Scheduler (Pinterest app)

The Plannery Pin Scheduler is an internal tool. It is used only by Plannery Templates, only with Plannery Templates' own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to other Pinterest users.

  • What it does: creates and schedules Pins of our own product images on our own boards, and reads analytics for our own Pins.
  • What it accesses: our own account's boards, Pins and Pin analytics, through the Pinterest API with our permission.
  • What it stores: the access token for our own account, kept privately on our own computer. It does not collect, store or share data about other Pinterest users.
  • Sharing: we do not sell or share any data from the Pinterest API with anyone.

You can read how Pinterest handles data in Pinterest's privacy policy.

Buying on Etsy

Orders are placed and paid on Etsy. Etsy shares with us only what we need to deliver your order and answer your messages, and we use it only for that. See Etsy's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask us to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us at the address above and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl).

Changes

If we start collecting new data, for example through a newsletter, we will update this page first and change the date at the top.
